Founding Crew & Artist Brief
The Ethos · Why This Room Exists
In English, people call it EDM. In Oatmeal, we say Electronic Digital Music.

Music was meant to be nourishment and healing—not just an algorithm chasing metrics or turning rooms into profit extractors. By trade, this operation comes from behind the rig: stage lighting, physical production, and building events from the first napkin sketch to the last garbage bag collected.

Rolled & Recorded is half Tiny Desk intimacy, half Boiler Room energy. A documented music party—not a sterile broadcast. Think a small room, real furniture, crowd right up on the table edge with no stage and no barricade, four cameras rolling, and sets that flow straight into a live podcast corner so the stream never stops.

We’re building safe, non-toxic kitchen energy—where working people can step away from the grind and enter their happy state. Getting fed by the sound, surrounded by good humans, with everyone offering something different to the community at the catering tent.

“This isn’t proof of concept. It’s proof of dopeness: ten die-hards in the room over a hundred strangers, four cameras rolling so the night lives past the night, and a network & workspace for the crew.”
The Look · Three Angles of the Room
Angle 01 · The Booth
No Stage · No Barricade
Over-the-Shoulder & Tally Lights
Camera tight behind the DJ’s hands on the mixer. Crowd packed right up to the wooden table edge. Four tripod cameras with glowing red tally lights framing the room, rubber duck on the deck, and 35mm analog grain.
Angle 02 · The Table
Sets Bleed Into Conversation
The Road Case & Podcast Corner
The DJ booth bleeds directly into the podcast corner. Broadcast boom mics draped in kandi bracelets, a black flight case serving as the side table, warm practical tungsten lamps, and steam rising like a fresh bowl of oats.
Angle 03 · First Light
One Hard Amber Beam + Cyan Rim
First Light & Hybrid Gear
“First Light” is the single hard amber beam slicing diagonally through room haze—no festival lasers, no stock club clichés. Analog/digital hybrid setups where a portable CD Walkman can patch straight into the mixer.
Pull Up A Chair · Crew Roles for Session 01
01 · Video & Broadcast
Four-Camera Shooters & Switch
We’re running four cameras in the round so every artist walks away with a complete multi-angle package of their set, and the live stream captures the room without feeling staged.
  • Cam A: Tight over-the-shoulder deck & hands angle
  • Cam B: Front/crowd-level room energy across the table
  • Cam C: Dedicated Podcast Corner two-shot for interviews
  • Cam D: Roaming / macro details (tally lights, gear, crowd)
02 · Podcast & Audio
Podcast Host & Sound Capture
Between sets, the stream doesn’t stop—it shifts straight to the podcast mics for 10-minute conversations with the artists and builders in the room.
  • Warm, unpretentious host energy (“the DJ after the set”)
  • Clean master board feed + room ambience + 2–3 boom mics
  • Zero dead air during 15–20 minute DJ changeovers
03 · Lighting, VJ & Tech
Lighting, Haze & Room Builders
Built by lighting folks, for lighting folks. The room is an active workspace where lighting presets, controllers, and atmospheric haze are part of the instrument.
  • Practical tungsten lamps + one hard amber beam through haze
  • Subtle cyan/UV accent on the decks & cables
  • Techies welcome: bring your controllers, visuals, or rig ideas
04 · Milwaukee → Chicago Corridor
Artists & Open Slot (15–20 Min Sets)
Five punchy 15–20 minute sets that bleed into each other. An expansion session where every performer leaves with real assets to push their project forward.
  • Free 4-camera video package of your full set—yours to keep
  • Custom lighting & mood built around your sound
  • Podcast-room interview + Info Table for your merch & links
